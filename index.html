About Us

A Place Where Recovery Begins

Hand Up Guiding Services provides a safe and supportive environment for Native Americans struggling with addiction. Our goal is to help individuals rebuild their lives through guidance, community support, and access to recovery resources.

Our mission is to empower those in need to achieve sustainable recovery while honoring their cultural heritage and traditions. We believe that every person deserves a hand up, not a hand out.

At Hand Up Guiding Services, we offer structured programs that focus on personal growth, wellness, and community integration.

Our Programs

Sober Living Support

A safe and structured environment for individuals working toward recovery, providing daily routines, mentorship, and community accountability.

Cultural Healing

Recovery support that respects and incorporates Native traditions, honoring heritage as a foundation for lasting wellness.

Life Skills

Guidance on employment, wellness, and rebuilding independence so you can move forward with confidence.

Family & Community Support

Guidance and counseling for families, helping loved ones understand recovery and create a strong support network.
